Hear how you come across on calls

A free Mac app that listens to your side of calls and gives you one tip after each. Runs on your Mac. Audio is never saved.

You spend hours a day on calls. Almost nobody tells you how you sounded.

Did you talk over people? Rush when it got tense?

Most tools that could tell you record everyone, upload it to the cloud and charge monthly.

Halen runs on your Mac. Do the 30-second voice check and it ignores every voice but yours. It turns the sound into a few numbers and throws the audio away.

No bot in the call, no audio saved, no account.

After each call you get one thing to try next time. You say how it went. After six rated calls it shows you which habits match your good ones.

Like a fitness ring, but for how you talk.

One thing to try next time.

Three scores and one tip after every call. Rate it in one drag.
