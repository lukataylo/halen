Privacy

What Halen EQ hears, what it keeps, and the only two things that ever touch the network.

Last updated 4 October 2026 · applies to Halen EQ 1.0

Audio never leaves your Mac, and with the voice check on, Halen EQ only analyses you. Everything runs on-device. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

What it hears

Your microphone, and only while a call is on (or you press Listen Now). It never saves or transcribes the other side of the call (see below).

If you set up the voice check, every second and a half of audio is compared with your voice print. Anything that isn’t you is wiped from memory before it’s analysed. If a moment of audio can’t be checked, it’s dropped rather than kept. If the voice model itself fails to load, Halen EQ tells you, and other voices near your mic may be included in that call’s numbers.

Knowing when they’re talking

To measure talk share and interruptions, Halen EQ watches the call app’s output volume: one loudness number at a time. The sound itself is thrown away instantly and never transcribed. macOS asks your permission for this; say no and Halen EQ still works, just with fewer listening stats.

What it keeps

DataKeptWhere
AudioNever. Analysed in memory, then discarded—
Scores and stats (pace, talk share, …)Until you delete themEncrypted on your Mac
Your own wordsOff by default. If you turn it on: 1, 7 or 30 daysEncrypted on your Mac
Your voice printUntil you delete itEncrypted on your Mac
Your ratings and notesUntil you delete themEncrypted on your Mac

Files are sealed with AES-GCM using a key that lives in your Mac’s keychain and never leaves this device. Saving your words is only possible once the voice check is on, so other people’s words can’t end up in it. Settings › Privacy › Forget Everything deletes it all, key included.

Network traffic

Three things, and nothing else:

  • Update check. Once a day Halen EQ fetches halen.dev/eq/appcast.xml. Updates are signed, and verified before they install. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Voice model download. The first time you set up the voice check, a small speaker-recognition model (about 30 MB) is downloaded from Hugging Face. It runs locally from then on.
  • Speech model. The first time, macOS may download Apple’s on-device speech model for your language. That’s Apple’s system download; your audio isn’t part of it.

No analytics, no crash reports, no telemetry. The code is open, so you can check every line.

Coaching text

The one-line takeaway after each call is written by Apple’s on-device model (Apple Intelligence), from the numbers only. If Apple Intelligence isn’t available, Halen EQ uses a simple built-in template instead. Nothing is sent to a server.

Recording laws

Halen EQ is designed to analyse only your own voice and to save no audio. Laws differ by place, and this isn’t legal advice. Speakers and shared rooms can let other voices reach your mic, so if you’re somewhere that requires everyone’s consent, tell people you use it.
